About Us

Dedicated to export logistics, delivering professional solutions for cross-border e-commerce

Company Profile

Shenzhen Quick & Perfect Express Co., Ltd. was founded in 2018 in Shenzhen, China. It is a service company focused on the export logistics sector, with products covering first-leg transportation and project logistics, specializing in professional cross-border e-commerce logistics solutions.

The company operates with multiple partner overseas warehouses in Vancouver, Montreal and Toronto, Canada. Overseas customs clearance and container devanning are fully controllable. For the overseas leg, we offer a variety of services including UPS, Canada Post, Purolator, LTL truck delivery and FTL direct truck delivery — providing customers with first-leg transportation, or a one-stop door-to-door logistics solution from the factory straight to overseas buyers.

We provide differentiated cross-border logistics solutions for customers on multiple e-commerce platforms. Air, sea and rail transport can be freely combined to deliver more professional and cost-effective plans.

The company owns its own logistics system, providing customers with full-process shipment tracking and handling.
