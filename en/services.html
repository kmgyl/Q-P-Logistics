Our Services

Air, sea and rail freely combined — professional, cost-effective one-stop logistics for cross-border e-commerce

01

Warehouse & Drop-shipping

Based at our Shenzhen warehouse, we offer convenient inbound warehousing: once your goods arrive, we pick, pack and drop-ship each order directly to your buyers — simple and worry-free.

  • One-stop receiving, inbound and storage at our Shenzhen warehouse
  • Order-based picking, packing and labelling
  • Drop-shipping single parcels straight to your buyers
  • Timely shipping updates, fully traceable orders
02

Air Freight Express

Leveraging stable capacity from quality carriers at home and abroad, our air freight express serves time-sensitive cargo — airport-to-airport or door-to-door.

  • Stable capacity, secured even in peak season
  • Same/next-day departures from major airport gateways
  • Solutions for battery and special-category cargo
  • End-to-end shipment tracking
03

Sea Freight

Backed by quality carrier resources at Shenzhen, Ningbo and other major ports, we provide stable weekly FCL and LCL services on US and Canada trade lanes at competitive rates.

  • Weekly FCL / LCL services on US & Canada lanes
  • General and special-category cargo accepted
  • One-stop destination clearance, pickup and delivery
  • Flexible shipping plans with visible capacity
04

Overseas Warehousing

Partner warehouses in Vancouver, Montreal and Toronto, Canada offer cross-border sellers one-stop overseas services including storage, transiting and relabeling.

  • Warehousing in Vancouver / Montreal / Toronto
  • Transit storage, relabeling, sorting and forwarding
  • Supports inbound and multi-channel distribution
  • Online inventory visibility
05

North America Last-mile Delivery

For the overseas leg we offer UPS, Canada Post, Purolator, LTL truck delivery and FTL direct truck delivery — a last-mile network covering all of North America.

  • Multiple channels: UPS / Canada Post / Purolator
  • LTL truck delivery and FTL direct truck delivery
  • Covering major warehouses and business addresses across North America
  • Trackable delivery times and full visibility
